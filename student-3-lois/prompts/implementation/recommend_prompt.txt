Task:
You are given a snapshot of current product stock levels, reorder levels, and suppliers.

Plan: identify products that are at or below their reorder level (low stock), and consider
which supplier they should be reordered from.
Act: produce a short, prioritised reorder recommendation list.
Observe: base every recommendation strictly on the supplied evidence below.
Adapt: if there are no low-stock products, recommend which product(s) are selling fastest
based on quantity relative to other items, or state that stock levels look healthy.

Output format:
- A short bullet list (max 5 bullets).
- Each bullet: product name, current quantity, reorder level, supplier to reorder from.
- Maximum 100 words total.
